IMX662-AAQR/AAQR1
Diagonal 6.45 mm (Type 1/2.8) CMOS Solid-state Image Sensor with Square Pixel for 
Color Cameras 
Description 
The IMX662-AAQR/AAQR1 is a diagonal 6.45 mm (Type 1/2.8) CMOS active pixel type solid-state image sensor 
with a square pixel array and 2.40 M effective pixels. This chip operates with analog 3.3 V, digital 1.1 V, and interface 
1.8 V triple power supply, and has low power consumption. High sensitivity, low dark current and no smear are 
achieved through the adoption of R, G and B primary color mosaic filters. This chip features an electronic shutter with 
variable charge-integration time. 
(Applications: Security cameras)
