IMX335LQN
Diagonal 6.52 mm (Type 1/2.8) CMOS Solid-state Image Sensor with Square Pixel for 
Color Cameras 
Description 
The IMX335LQN is a diagonal 6.52 mm (Type 1/2.8) CMOS active pixel type solid-state image sensor with a square 
pixel array and 5.14 M effective pixels. This chip operates with analog 2.9 V, digital 1.2 V, and interface 1.8 V triple 
power supply, and has low power consumption. High sensitivity, low dark current and no smear are achieved through 
the adoption of R, G and B primary color mosaic filters. This chip features an electronic shutter with variable 
charge-integration time. 
(Applications: Surveillance cameras, FA cameras, Industrial cameras)

◆ CMOS active pixel type dots
